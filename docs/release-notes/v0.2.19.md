# PCR Market Watch v0.2.19

**English** below · **简体中文**见下方

## What's new

- **Dark blue-slate theme** on every page, including charts and tooltips.
- **PCR snapshot strip** on the homepage, with TOTAL as the lead card: daily PCR, the signed change versus the prior session, 5/20/60-session PCR and the 20-session percentile, each with its own data date.
- **Clearer summary table:** every column kept, a sticky first column, decimal-aligned numbers, and Cboe/market-reference names that open the matching market history.
- **Compact floating quote bar** after scrolling, without covering headings, anchors or focused controls.
- **Language switch** shows English and 中文 together on every page.
- PCR trend lines are coloured by importance (daily gray, 5-session teal, 20-session blue, 60-session amber).

No API, calculation, database, credential or permission changes.

## 简体中文

### 更新内容

- **蓝灰深色主题**覆盖全部页面，包括图表与悬浮提示。
- **首页 PCR 快照指标条**，TOTAL 为主卡片：单日 PCR、较前一交易日的带符号变化、5／20／60 日 PCR 与 20 日分位，各自标注数据日期。
- **摘要宽表更清楚：** 保留全部列，首列固定，数字按小数点对齐，点击 Cboe／市场参考名称可打开对应的市场历史。
- **滚动后悬浮行情收为紧凑条**，不遮挡标题、锚点或获得焦点的控件。
- **语言切换**在每个页面同时显示 English 与中文。
- PCR 趋势线按重要性配色（单日灰、5 日青绿、20 日蓝、60 日琥珀）。

不改 API、计算、数据库、凭据或权限。
