# PCR Market Watch v0.2.24

**English** below · **简体中文**见下方

## What's new

- **Clearer Gamma "distribution by strike" chart.** In night mode the grid and zero lines were brighter than the data, the Call/Put bars were thin and dim, and the net-contribution diamonds blurred into a white band along zero. Now:
  - Call (above zero) and Put (below zero) bars share one column, so each bar is about twice as wide on dense chains; where both have the same sign, the taller one is drawn first so both stay visible.
  - Night mode uses brighter bar, diamond and underlying-price colours, with dimmer grid and zero lines.
  - Diamonds are larger and outlined in the panel colour; those sitting on the zero line are drawn smaller.
  - The Call wall, Put wall and underlying-price labels have a halo in the panel colour, so they stay legible when they coincide.
- Day mode keeps its colours and gets the same wider bars, diamonds and label halos.

No API, calculation, database, credential, permission or security-policy changes; chart values are unchanged.

## 简体中文

### 更新内容

- **伽马“按行权价分布”图更清楚。** 夜间模式下原先网格线和零线比数据还亮，Call／Put 柱又细又暗，净贡献菱形在零线上糊成一条白带。现在：
  - Call（零线以上）与 Put（零线以下）柱共用一列，行权价密集时每根柱子宽约一倍；两者同号时先画较长的一根，两根都看得见。
  - 夜间模式的柱子、菱形与现价颜色更亮，网格线与零线调暗。
  - 菱形放大并以面板底色描边；紧贴零线的菱形缩小。
  - Call 墙、Put 墙与现价标签加了面板底色描边，三者重叠时也能看清。
- 日间模式保持原配色，同样获得更宽的柱子、更清楚的菱形与标签。

不改 API、计算、数据库、凭据、权限或安全策略；图表数值不变。
