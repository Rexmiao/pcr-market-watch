Give each reading
historical context.

Historical percentiles compare daily, 20-session and 60-session PCR separately. Each uses up to 252 valid observations before the selected data date, with the actual sample size displayed.

Prior-year comparison shows one selected measure as two lines aligned by calendar month and day. Missing records remain blank, and the summary shows both the target and actual comparison dates.

Source, timing
and methodology.

Volume, open interest and price quotes are labeled separately, so every reading has a verifiable meaning.

Volume PCR
Daily PCR is put volume divided by call volume. The 20/60-session measures use totals within each window; insufficient data remains blank.
Cboe / OCC
Cboe categories follow the scope of its published reports. Stock and ETF volumes come from OCC. Full-day observations follow each source’s publication schedule.
OI snapshots
Open-interest PCR is displayed separately and does not refresh with volume. Symbols without a reliable snapshot remain blank, never zero.
Supporting quotes
Three major U.S. stock indices, crude oil and gold provide market context. Optional monitoring uses local moomoo or separately authorized WebAPI, with distinct coverage for partial scans, provider summaries and unusual activity records.
Gamma / Max Pain
Windows v0.2.24 includes Gamma and Max Pain references, per-expiry archives within 30 days and historical comparison. Scope, query time and source time are labeled separately. Model estimates are not observed dealer inventory or price targets. Version v0.2.3 remains a historical release.
